How this works

  • Every question is one where the candidates actually differ — picked by spread, not vibes.
  • Per-question importance slider; defaults to medium so you can just answer.
  • Two separate scores per candidate: policy match and personal fit. Never blended.
  • Every position links to a primary-source quote. Flag anything that's wrong.

We log anonymized response counts to build a public stats page. No tracking cookies, no IPs stored.
